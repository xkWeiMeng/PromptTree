## Scope

This notice applies to PromptTree Web, mobile, and browser extension usage, and explains service availability, data privacy, and support channels.

## Service Availability Notice

PromptTree is currently built and maintained by an early-stage startup team. We continuously improve system reliability, but due to network conditions, infrastructure maintenance, or release updates, services may occasionally experience short downtime, sync delays, or temporary disconnections.

We will do our best to monitor, investigate, and resolve incidents, and restore service as quickly as possible.

## Data Privacy and Security

We take user privacy and data security seriously and apply necessary technical and operational protections, including but not limited to:

- Encrypted transmission (HTTPS)
- Access control and authentication mechanisms
- Ongoing security and reliability improvements

We will not use your content for unauthorized purposes.

## Data Retention and Risk Notice

We make best efforts to minimize data-loss risk; however, no internet service can eliminate all failure risks. For critical content, we recommend keeping an additional local backup copy.
